HEXES
first playable slice
Booting the rig
Phone: hold anywhere to walk toward your finger, or tap MOVE for a thumb joystick. Tap the numbered cast buttons and VENT; HEX edits your hexes at a workbench.
Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move in any direction, 1 to 4 press the cast buttons (Space is button 1), R vents heat, Tab or E edits hexes at a workbench.
Strikes are automatic. Shove things into pits. Find the ▼ stairs.
Your progress saves on this device. Dying drops you back a few floors and leaves your newest parts in a salvage cache.
New: CAST BUTTONS AND WORKBENCHES. Every on-cast hex has its own numbered button, so two cast hexes are two buttons. Hexes are rewired only at a workbench, a purple console beside where you arrive and beside the stairs. There, the BUTTON switch moves the hex on show to another button; put two hexes on one button and one press fires both.
FRAMES. Every slot holds a frame that bends the part in it: Doubler fires an effect twice, Kill-lock fires again after a kill, Prism borrows the type before it, Leech pays you Power, and more. Frames drop from foes, elites most. Tap HEX, then FRAMES, pick a frame, tap a slot: green slots take it. Fitting one scraps the frame that was there. TEST unlocks every frame to try.
DARKNESS. You only see as far as your rig's lamp reaches, about six hexes, and walls block it. Ground you've explored stays dimly remembered, and foes outside the light are hidden. Overheating or venting browns the lamp out for a moment. Your hexes only aim at foes you can see, so light is reach: pick up LENS drops to make your lamp reach further for good.
LOOK. Walls stand up off the floor, the floor has panels and grates, and the station changes color every few floors as you go deeper. Foes are little machines with glowing eyes that turn to face you, breathe and flinch when hit. Hits flash and ring, big ones and hits on you shake the screen, and the edge goes red when you're hurt. Power and Heat sit on arcs round your rig, and Heat wraps the CAST button.
BIGGER FLOORS. Every floor down is bigger than the last, up to depth 17. A minimap in the corner charts where you’ve been and rings the stairs once you’ve seen them.
SOUND. Every sound is synthesized from the same numbers as the effects: each damage type has its own voice, harder hits hit harder, casts climb with their Power and hiss with their Heat. Sound starts on your first tap; the speaker button by MENU mutes, the menu sets the volume.
EFFECTS. Hits, areas and casts now animate from their numbers: each damage type moves its own way, harder hits are bigger, and every cast shows its Power pulled in and its Heat vented out.
EXAMPLE BUILDS. Tap HEX, then TEST, and pick one of nine example builds to play it (Brawler, Grenade, Tripwire, Deep Freeze...). MY BUILD puts your own build back.
Triggers and board banks: Two rare triggers: CHANNEL runs its hex over and over while you hold CAST, and WATCH (exotic) fires on its own when a condition or a library hex finds someone, at a little Heat per check. Charges and spend gates can now use the board's shared banks X and Y (tap the center plate to switch). A hex you equip needs an effect, charge or call before it fires.
Conditions: Tap a gate in a hex, then tap it again: you build its test from logic tiles (hull, crowd, heat, less than, and...). Foes who pass go one way, the rest the other. You start with five tiles; more drop as loot (orange squares). TEST unlocks them all.
Library calls: A module on the board is a library hex. Pick a Call in a hex and tap it again (or the center plate) until it names a module: that module now runs right there, and the slot after the call acts on whoever it returns. Drop a multiplier from your pack on the call to add it as an option.
Docking board: tap HEX, then BOARD. Turn and move hexes so one's orange way out faces another's green way in. TEST opens the whole board and every shape.
Parts only go where they do something: pick one up and the slots it works in turn green.
