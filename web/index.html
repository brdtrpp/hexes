HEXES
first playable slice
Booting the rig
Phone: hold anywhere to walk toward your finger, or tap MOVE for a thumb joystick. Tap CAST and VENT, HEX to edit your hexes.
Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move in any direction, Space casts Arc Lance, R vents heat, Tab or E edits hexes.
Strikes are automatic. Shove things into pits. Find the ▼ stairs.
Your progress saves on this device. Dying drops you back a few floors and leaves your newest parts in a salvage cache.
New: THE DOCKING BOARD. Tap HEX, then BOARD. Turn and move hexes so one's orange way out faces another's green way in, and its chain flows on into the next hex. Build modules (hexes with no trigger) on open cells; more cells open as you go deeper. TEST opens the whole board and every shape.
Parts only go where they do something: pick one up and the slots it works in turn green.
