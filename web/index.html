HEXES
first playable slice
Booting the rig
NEW: CLOAK IS SELF ONLY. Cloak never hurts anyone now: it hides you, and your next hit out of it lands double. Any cloak damage parts you already had turned into cloaks, so nothing left your pack or locker. Try HEX, TEST, MORE: Rogue (Vanish, then Backstab).
NEW: KEEP ONE. The first time you take a floor's stairs, pick 1 of 3 cards (4 on every 5th floor): a free grid cell, a part, a frame, signals or Data. TAKE NONE banks a little Data. A new save also picks 1 of 3 starting rigs, each with its own first hex and Power spread. Pink gunners stuck behind their pack now back off, swap places or sidestep instead of shooting point blank.
NEW: CLASS PARTS. Forms (bolt, beam, aura, nova, dash, detonate, zone, turret, trap, drone), wrecks that fallen foes leave for a while, raised minions, taunt, thorns, haste, blink, stances you tap on and off, and costs paid in hull or wrecks. They sit on the grid from ring 12 out, drop as loot, and the ladder teaches them on floors 17 to 25. Try HEX, TEST, MORE: Rogue, Necromancer, Engineer and Warden.
FIXED: THE GRID FOG IS BACK. TEST in the editor no longer clears the grid. On the GRID tab, TEST now lifts the fog only while it's lit, and leaving the grid brings it back.
NEW: MORE CHARACTER. The camera leans back so the station has depth. Each damage kind has its own shape (plasma burns, cryo shatters into crystal and frost, kinetic cracks the deck, nanites swarm, drones lock on, cloak shimmers), and bigger hexes add structure, not just size. Foes now hunt in packs: one that spots you calls the rest, they surround you, hurt ones fall back, and pink gunners from depth 3 keep their distance and aim before they shoot.
NEW: THE SKILL GRID. Spend Data on a fogged hex grid at the workbench: HEX > GRID, tap a cell, hold BUY. Saves from before this start fresh at depth 1.
Phone: hold anywhere to walk toward your finger, or tap MOVE for a thumb joystick. Tap the numbered cast buttons and VENT; HEX edits your hexes at a workbench.
Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move in any direction, 1 to 4 press the cast buttons (Space is button 1), R vents heat, Tab or E edits hexes at a workbench.
Strikes are automatic. Shove things into pits. Find the ▼ stairs.
Your progress saves on this device. Dying drops you back a few floors and leaves your newest parts in a salvage cache.
NEW: THE SKILL GRID. Your skills now come from a huge fogged hexagon grid rolled for your save. Kills, new depths and salvage pay DATA. At a workbench, HEX > GRID shows the grid: tap a cell next to what you own and hold BUY to take it. Parts you buy go in your LOCKER (a tab beside PACK). You only see the cells next to yours; rarer ones glow from further out. Rings open as the lessons below do. Dying lets go of a few outer cells (buy them back at half price) and drops unbanked Data in your salvage crate. Loot drops are now rare extras. Saves from before this start fresh.
THE GAME TEACHES ITSELF. You start with one small hex on button 1. Each floor has a glowing pickup near where you arrive, with a beacon you can see through the dark: walk over it and a card says what it gives you. Effects and the workbench on floor 1, targets on 2, a second hex on 3, multipliers on 4, Mend on 5, frames on 6, shapes and hex 3 on 7, gates on 9, charges on 11, calls, hex 4 and the board on 13, and everything else from 15. Loot only drops what you've been taught, and what you learn stays even when you die. Saves from before this start fresh at depth 1. In the editor, ? explains any part in one sentence, and TEST still unlocks everything.
New: SUSTAIN PARTS. MEND restores hull (on a kill it's lifesteal), SHIELD soaks the next few hits, PURGE dumps Heat for Power. Mend and Shield fire weaker if you fire them fast. They drop uncommon or better once you've had your first Mend, the depth 5 lesson. Try HEX, TEST, Field Medic at the workbench.
CAST BUTTONS AND WORKBENCHES. Every on-cast hex has its own numbered button, so two cast hexes are two buttons. Hexes are rewired only at a workbench, a purple console beside where you arrive and beside the stairs. There, the BUTTON switch moves the hex on show to another button; put two hexes on one button and one press fires both.
FRAMES. Every slot holds a frame that bends the part in it: Doubler fires an effect twice, Kill-lock fires again after a kill, Prism borrows the type before it, Leech pays you Power, and more. Frames drop from foes, elites most. Tap HEX, then FRAMES, pick a frame, tap a slot: green slots take it. Fitting one scraps the frame that was there. TEST unlocks every frame to try.
DARKNESS. You only see as far as your rig's lamp reaches, about six hexes, and walls block it. Ground you've explored stays dimly remembered, and foes outside the light are hidden. Overheating or venting browns the lamp out for a moment. Your hexes only aim at foes you can see, so light is reach: pick up LENS drops to make your lamp reach further for good.
LOOK. Walls stand up off the floor, the floor has panels and grates, and the station changes color every few floors as you go deeper. Foes are little machines with glowing eyes that turn to face you, breathe and flinch when hit. Hits flash and ring, big ones and hits on you shake the screen, and the edge goes red when you're hurt. Power and Heat sit on arcs round your rig, and Heat wraps the CAST button.
BIGGER FLOORS. Every floor down is bigger than the last, up to depth 17. A minimap in the corner charts where you’ve been and rings the stairs once you’ve seen them.
SOUND. Every sound is synthesized from the same numbers as the effects: each damage type has its own voice, harder hits hit harder, casts climb with their Power and hiss with their Heat. Sound starts on your first tap; the speaker button by MENU mutes, the menu sets the volume.
EFFECTS. Hits, areas and casts now animate from their numbers: each damage type moves its own way, harder hits are bigger, and every cast shows its Power pulled in and its Heat vented out.
EXAMPLE BUILDS. Tap HEX, then TEST, and pick one of ten example builds to play it (Brawler, Grenade, Tripwire, Field Medic...). MY BUILD puts your own build back.
Triggers and board banks: Two rare triggers: CHANNEL runs its hex over and over while you hold CAST, and WATCH (exotic) fires on its own when a condition or a library hex finds someone, at a little Heat per check. Charges and spend gates can now use the board's shared banks X and Y (tap the center plate to switch). A hex you equip needs an effect, charge or call before it fires.
Conditions: Tap a gate in a hex, then tap it again: you build its test from logic tiles (hull, crowd, heat, less than, and...). Foes who pass go one way, the rest the other. You start with five tiles; more drop as loot (orange squares). TEST unlocks them all.
Library calls: A module on the board is a library hex. Pick a Call in a hex and tap it again (or the center plate) until it names a module: that module now runs right there, and the slot after the call acts on whoever it returns. Drop a multiplier from your pack on the call to add it as an option.
Docking board: tap HEX, then BOARD. Turn and move hexes so one's orange way out faces another's green way in. TEST opens the whole board and every shape.
Parts only go where they do something: pick one up and the slots it works in turn green.
