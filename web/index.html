first playable slice
Booting the rig
NEW: DRAG TO BUILD. Drag a piece to move it, drag one from your pack onto the board, or drag it onto the tray to set it aside. Pieces join where they touch; tap the bar between two pieces to step YES, NO or a rung. The trigger can go anywhere, and every board has LOOP, TWIN and SURGE pads scattered out in all six directions: the piece on one gets its bonus. Drag empty board to look around.
Phone: hold anywhere to walk toward your finger, or tap MOVE for a thumb joystick. Pinch with two fingers to zoom. Tap the numbered cast buttons and VENT; HEX edits your hexes at a workbench.
Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move in any direction, 1 to 4 press the cast buttons (Space is button 1), R vents heat, Tab or E edits hexes at a workbench. Mouse wheel or + and - zoom, 0 resets.
Your progress saves on this device and carries across updates. Dying drops you back a few floors and leaves your newest parts in a salvage cache.
